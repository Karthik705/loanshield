The LoanShield system

The app is one part. People do the rest.

Five stages run in a loop. Teal stages are carried by this app; grey ones by self-help groups, legal-aid clinics, volunteers and regulators.

    Hotspots found in Reform decide where the next Prevent camps run.

    It answers fear with facts

    Other tools label a message as spam. LoanShield tells the person what the threat really means and what the law says about it.

    Every threat strengthens the case

    Each message becomes sealed evidence, a law broken and a link to the operator. Being harassed starts producing proof.

    It follows the money, not the app name

    Ten rebranded apps that use one UPI ID become one operator, and only after three different people report it.
