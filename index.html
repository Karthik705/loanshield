If a photo of you was shared

This photo is fake. Making it is the crime, not being in it.You don’t have to look at it again, and you don’t have to do this alone.
  1. 1
    Don’t open it, reply or pay.

    Paying never makes them delete it. It only brings new demands.

  2. 2
    Tell your contacts it’s fake.

    People believe you when they hear it from you first.

  3. 3
    Ask each platform to take it down.

    Platforms must act within 2 hours of a complaint about morphed intimate images (IT Rules, as amended in 2026).

    On WhatsApp nobody can delete it from other phones: report and block the sender, and ask your contacts to delete it and not forward it.

  4. 4
    Stop it being uploaded again.

    StopNCII.org makes a fingerprint of the image on your own phone. The image never leaves it, and partner platforms block matching copies.

  5. 5
    Let a counsellor do it for you.

    A trained NGO volunteer handles the takedowns, so you never have to see it again. You can ask for a woman volunteer.

Ask a volunteer to call you

A trained volunteer from our partner NGO calls you, in your language, and goes through every step with you. Only the LoanShield volunteer team sees this.

Best time to call

Our volunteers will never ask you for money, an OTP or your bank details.
