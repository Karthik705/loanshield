The problem

Easy loans. Brutal collection.

Illegal instant-loan apps target low-income, first-time borrowers, then turn the phone into a weapon.

Contact blackmail

Your family and boss get the threats.

Morphed images

Fake photos, sent to everyone you know.

Fake legal notices

Arrest warnings with no court behind them.

Hidden cost

Fees cut upfront, full repayment in 7 days.

Why bans alone don't work

Blocking targets the app name, the cheapest thing a scammer can change. The money still lands in the same UPI accounts, and the same call centre reads the same script. LoanShield is built around those links.

      Six things only LoanShield does

      Detect

      Links rebrands by the money trail

      Groups app names by shared UPI IDs, phone numbers and scripts, so a ban can target the operator instead of the name.

      Respond

      Turns lone victims into one case

      When three or more people report the same operator, their evidence becomes one bundled case for the cyber cell.

      Protect

      Evidence that proves it wasn't edited

      A hash chain records each item's SHA-256, the value a Section 63 certificate asks for in court [8].

      Prevent

      Shows the real cost before you borrow

      Converts "₹5,000 in 7 days" into a yearly rate and checks it against RBI and Play Store rules.

      Protect

      Disarms the contact list first

      Contact Shield warns family and colleagues in English, Hindi or Tamil before the harassers reach them, so the blackmail loses its power.

      Respond

      Names the rule each message breaks

      Every saved threat is matched to RBI's recovery rules, the BNS or the IT Act, and late-night calls are checked against RBI's 8 am to 7 pm window.

        Under the hood

        What runs where.

        Your messages stay on your phone. Only the harassers' identifiers leave it, and only when you choose to report.

        Requirements

        • FR1Show the yearly cost and rule violations of any loan offer in under 10 seconds.
        • FR2Classify a shared message by threat type, and extract UPI IDs, numbers and links.
        • FR3Store evidence in a hash chain that detects any later edit or deletion.
        • FR4Link reports into operators and publish only at 3 or more distinct reporters.
        • FR5Produce a complaint packet the user files themselves, naming the rules each message breaks.
        • FR6Draft a warning the user sends their own contacts, in English, Hindi or Tamil.
        • NFR1Message text never leaves the device. Consent before any report. Follows the DPDP Act and Rules [9].
        • NFR2Works offline, in English and romanised Hindi first, and on basic phones through WhatsApp or IVR.
        • NFR3Passes Play Store review: no SMS, call-log or contacts permission.

        Android permissions

        PermissionUsedWhy
        READ_SMS, READ_CALL_LOGNoPlay restricts both to default SMS and dialer apps. Text arrives by Share.
        READ_CONTACTSNoWe protect contacts; we don't collect them.

        Testing

        Does the scanner work?

        We wrote 50 messages, 25 harassing and 25 ordinary, and ran the scanner on them. These results are computed live in your browser from the same code the toolkit uses.

        A message counts as harassment at a score of 40 or more.

        Where it was wrong, and why

            What this does and doesn't prove

            • The team wrote the test messages and tuned one weight after the first run, so these scores are optimistic.
            • The scanner leans toward flagging. People only share messages that already worry them, and a missed threat costs more than a false alarm.
            • Real accuracy gets measured in the pilot, with consenting users marking each result right or wrong.
            • Also checked in code: the yearly-rate formula, the hash chain catching an edited item, and the graph merging 8 reports into 3 operators.

            Pilot

            90 days in one district.

            Partners: one SHG federation, one law college legal-aid clinic, and the district cyber cell. Students run it.

            Weeks 1–4

            Teach and check

            • Truth Check camps at SHG meetings
            • Translate the scanner's word list with members
            • Agree a referral route to the cyber cell
            Weeks 5–8

            Scan and save

            • Volunteers help victims scan and save evidence
            • Users mark each scan right or wrong
            • First reports enter the scam graph
            Weeks 9–12

            Bundle and report

            • Bundled cases go to the cyber cell
            • Publish the first dashboard
            • Write the findings brief for RBI and MeitY
